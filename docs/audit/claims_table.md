# Claims and contribution classification — Tasks 4–5

## What counts as a contribution here

The defensible claim is **project-level reproduction engineering plus a controlled local empirical analysis**. There is no established genuinely new resampling method. Category 3 below means newly conducted/documented analysis within this project, not first-in-literature novelty. Individual personal authorship of earlier team work is UNVERIFIED by this audit; do not replace “the project provides” with an unsupported claim that you personally invented or authored every component. Evidence: `scripts/run_sshr_small_study.py:31–38`, `src/sshr_evaluation.py:260–271`, [audit_protocol.md](audit_protocol.md).

## What the evidence does not decide

Lack of an observed benefit is not proof of no possible benefit, and poor pseudo-label precision is not an isolated causal explanation of the entire U effect: access to U also changes clustering, cleaning and fallback seeds. Evidence: `src/sshr.py:103–118`; `docs/SSHR_METHOD_DECISIONS.md:242`; both `ablation.csv` files.

The small numerical gaps between mean leaders are not evidence of significance. Original papers' significance tests cannot be transferred to this different two-dataset protocol. Evidence: `scripts/run_sshr_small_study.py:31–38`; `docs/SSHR_METHOD_DECISIONS.md:264–266`; [audit_protocol.md](audit_protocol.md).
